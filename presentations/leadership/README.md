# WorkTrace Leadership Presentation

This folder contains the leadership-facing presentation material for WorkTrace.

## Audience

Technical leadership evaluating:

- the problem WorkTrace addresses
- how WorkTrace differs from existing Jira / AI-agent integrations
- the proposed technical approach
- privacy and risk controls
- the MVP and pilot strategy
- the decision / sponsorship required to proceed

## Presentation Objective

The deck should allow leadership to quickly understand:

1. **Why the problem exists now** — engineering work increasingly happens inside AI coding sessions.
2. **What is currently lost** — Jira can become an incomplete representation of actual work.
3. **What WorkTrace does** — reconciles AI-assisted work evidence against Jira.
4. **How it works technically** — deterministic evidence first, AI only where needed, human confirmation before write-back.
5. **Why the first step is low risk** — the proposed V0 is local and read-only.
6. **What leadership is being asked to approve** — a small pilot focused on validating matching quality.
